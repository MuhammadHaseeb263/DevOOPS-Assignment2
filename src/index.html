Why we exist

Nobody in this city should have to choose between a good book and a full wallet. Readers’ Haven opened to give every resident a free place to read, study and borrow, whatever their age or budget.

We keep shelves of fiction, non-fiction, children’s books and reference works. We also run e-books, study rooms and free Wi-Fi, so you can use the library even when you cannot visit in person.
